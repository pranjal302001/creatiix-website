Logo, colour, type and interface rules for the CREATIIX website and anything made to match it.

01 · Logo

The origami bird.

The mark is a folded-paper bird built from six pieces. It always appears in brand orange with a peach wing tip. Only the wordmark changes colour with the background.

On dark · ivory wordmark · logo-on-dark.png

On light · charcoal wordmark · logo-on-light.png

Mark only

Horizontal (navbar)

App icon / favicon

Wordmark on gradient

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the wing tip.
  • Minimum size: mark 24px tall; full logo 120px wide.
  • Use the ivory wordmark on dark and the charcoal wordmark on light.
  • Use the supplied transparent PNG files in public/brand/.

Don't

  • Recolour the mark or put a gradient on it.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Place the orange mark on orange or peach backgrounds.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font.

Used on primary buttons, highlighted words, "Core focus" badges, one process card and the contact panel. Text on the gradient is always Charcoal. On light backgrounds, gradient text stops at Orange so it stays readable.

04 · Interface

One curve, frosted glass.

Every button, card, input, badge and panel uses the same 20px corner. Cards are frosted glass sitting over soft orange glows.

Corner radius

20px

Everywhere. Circles (avatars, dots) are the only exception.

Glass card

Fill: white 5.5% (dark) / 50% (light)
Border: 1px white 13% / 90%
Blur: 22px, saturate 160%
Shadow: 0 18px 40px black 18%

Glow

Radial Flameburst → Orange, blur 90px.

  • Smooth scrolling; sections fade up as they enter.
  • Cards lean toward the mouse (3D tilt, desktop only).
  • The 3D origami bird sways gently in the hero (desktop only).
  • Short logo reveal on first visit, about one second.
  • Animations stop for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

06 · Voice

Plain words, confident tone.

We say

  • What the client gets, in simple language.
  • "One team for AI, apps, design and marketing."
  • Timelines are "as per requirement".
  • "Let's talk" and "Start your project" on buttons.
  • Examples labelled "Concept" until real work exists.

We avoid

  • Prices or packages on the website.
  • Fixed delivery dates or guarantees.
  • Invented clients, numbers or case studies.
  • Jargon without a plain explanation.
  • Headings in all capitals.
